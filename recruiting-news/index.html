What to check before attending an event

Use your university career portal and the employer's careers website to confirm the date, time zone, location, registration deadline and eligible graduation years. Some sessions require advance registration or restrict attendance to particular schools.

Turn recruiting updates into a useful conversation

Before a presentation, research the team and prepare two questions about its work. Afterwards, write a short, specific introduction to a relevant professional. Keep track of whom you contacted and whether a follow-up would be appropriate.
