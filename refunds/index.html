Refunds & cancellation

Effective and last updated September 12, 2026

Automatic renewal

Starter, Pro, and Power subscriptions renew monthly. The Recruiting Season Pass renews every three months. The payment provider identified at checkout charges the payment method associated with your subscription at the beginning of each new billing period until you cancel.

Canceling a subscription

You may cancel at any time using Manage billing in Orbit or the manage-subscription link in your receipt or subscription email. You can also request cancellation by emailing lw3405@nyu.edu. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period and prevents future renewal charges. You keep access through the end of that period unless the payment provider's applicable terms or applicable law requires a different result.

Refund requests

Except where required by law or stated below, completed purchases and renewal charges are non-refundable, including for unused credits or unused time. If you believe you were charged incorrectly, did not receive the purchased service, or experienced a material technical defect, request help using the payment provider's support link in your receipt or email lw3405@nyu.edu with your Orbit account email and approximate charge date.

Mandatory consumer rights

This policy does not limit rights that cannot be waived under applicable law. Some consumers may have a statutory withdrawal period for an initial digital-service purchase. Starting to use or benefit from a digital service may affect that right where the law permits and where the required consent was given at checkout. The payment provider identified at checkout will evaluate eligible requests under its applicable buyer terms and refund policy.

How refunds are processed

The payment provider identified in your receipt processes approved refunds. Refunds are ordinarily returned to the original payment method. Processing time may depend on the payment provider, your payment method, and your financial institution.

Protect your payment information. Never email a complete card number, security code, password, or bank credential. Your account email and charge date are normally enough to locate a transaction.

Contact

For product or billing questions, contact lw3405@nyu.edu. For payment, receipt, cancellation, or refund processing, use the payment provider's support link in your receipt or subscription email.
