Good to know.

Do I need to install OpenClaw, Node.js or an AI model?

No. Orbit's AI execution stays on our server. Your Chrome Extension only connects the tabs you approve.

Can I close the Orbit website during a Run?

Yes, once a task has been submitted the cloud queue owns its state. Keep Chrome and the authorized LinkedIn/Gmail tabs open while browser actions are needed. Closing those tabs or putting your computer to sleep can pause the task.

What if Chrome shows a browser debugging warning?

That warning is issued by Chrome whenever the Extension controls an authorized tab. You can stop Orbit from the Extension or click Chrome's Cancel control.

What is the difference between Chrome and Desktop?

Chrome uses a small Extension plus the cloud Agent. Desktop is a separate downloadable app. Both should share your Orbit account and tasks as product integration is completed.
