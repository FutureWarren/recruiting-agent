# Orbit — Personal AI Cold Email Agent
## Approved positioning and promotional copy · 2026-11-16

### Master positioning
**Orbit — Your Personal AI Cold Email Agent.**

**One-liner:** Find alumni and professionals, discover their work emails, and prepare personalized cold email drafts in Gmail. You review and approve before sending.

**Audience:** Students and early-career job seekers who want to build professional relationships through thoughtful cold emailing.

**What Orbit does today:** Research relevant people, attempt work-email lookup, prepare personalized Gmail drafts, and support user-reviewed outreach. The user controls approval, pause and stop. A contact email, a drafted message or a reply is never guaranteed.

### Marketing truth guardrails
- **Do say:** `personal AI cold email agent`, `research professionals`, `attempt to discover work emails`, `personalized Gmail drafts`, `review before sending`, `computer-use browser workflow`.
- **Do not say:** `guaranteed replies`, `guaranteed offers`, `every email is verified`, `100 free emails`, `unlimited automatic sending`, `Auto Apply is live`, `Chrome Extension is publicly available`.
- Do not suggest that example employers endorse Orbit.
- Never imply that downloading the desktop app or entering an email authorizes sending third-party outreach.
- Stay consistent with the live supported Mac/Windows platforms and existing Credits disclosures. No new prices or features are being launched with this copy-only change.

### Deployment scope
Website copy and site metadata only, plus tests and this kit. No changes to mobile handoff execution, installer resolution, billing, outreach approvals, user account data, or any desktop/backend deployment.
