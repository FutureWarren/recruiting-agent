# Orbit — Personal AI Recruiting Agent
## Current positioning and promotional copy · updated 2026-11-26

### Master positioning
**Orbit — Your Personal AI Recruiting Agent.**

**One-liner:** Research relevant professionals, find work emails, prepare personalized outreach, track replies, and follow up across your recruiting workflow. You review and approve before anything is sent.

**Audience:** Students and early-career job seekers who want one controlled workflow for recruiting research, networking outreach, reply tracking, and follow-up.

**What Orbit does today:** Research relevant people, attempt work-email lookup, prepare personalized outreach, detect replies, and support follow-up workflows. The user controls approval, pause and stop. A contact email, a drafted message, a reply or a recruiting outcome is never guaranteed.

### Marketing truth guardrails
- **Do say:** `personal AI recruiting agent`, `research professionals`, `attempt to discover work emails`, `personalized outreach`, `reply tracking`, `follow-up workflows`, `review before sending`, `computer-use browser workflow`.
- **Do not say:** `guaranteed replies`, `guaranteed offers`, `every email is verified`, `100 free emails`, `unlimited automatic sending`, `Auto Apply is live`, `Chrome Extension is publicly available`.
- Do not suggest that example employers endorse Orbit.
- Never imply that downloading the desktop app or entering an email authorizes sending third-party outreach.
- Stay consistent with the live supported Mac/Windows platforms and existing Credits disclosures. No new prices or features are being launched with this copy-only change.

### Deployment scope
Website copy and site metadata only, plus tests and this kit. No changes to mobile handoff execution, installer resolution, billing, outreach approvals, user account data, or any desktop/backend deployment.
