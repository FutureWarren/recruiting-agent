How can we help?

Account, billing, credits, and product support
Email Orbit support

Include your Orbit account email, app version, and a short description of what happened.

future@angelic.ai

Billing and subscriptions

Manage or cancel an active subscription through the Paddle customer portal linked in your receipt or subscription email. For payment processing, receipts, or a refund request, visit paddle.net.

Credits did not update

Return to Orbit after checkout and reopen the account panel. Orbit refreshes your plan and credit balance when the app regains focus. If the balance still does not update after a short wait, email us with your Orbit account email and approximate payment time. Do not send card details.

Account access

Use the account menu in the upper-right corner of Orbit to sign out and sign back in with Google. Your locally stored campaign work remains on your device when you sign out.

Reporting a product issue

Tell us what you asked Orbit to do, what you expected, what happened, and whether the issue repeats. A screenshot is helpful, but remove sensitive information before sending it.
