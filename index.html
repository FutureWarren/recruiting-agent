Orbit supports Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 11+. Google Chrome, LinkedIn, and Gmail are required.

  1. Install Orbit macOS

    Open the .dmg and drag Orbit into Applications.

  2. Open Orbit

    Launch Orbit from Applications. The first launch may take a moment while macOS verifies the Apple-notarized app.

    1. Make sure Chrome is installed

      Orbit uses a separate Chrome window to keep its work apart from everyday browsing. Get Chrome.

    2. Make it yours

      Add your background and résumé, connect your accounts, describe your task, then review and start.
