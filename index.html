Your AI recruiting agent

Tell it where you want to work. It finds the right people and does the outreach.

macOS · free · no account needed
What it does

It networks for you, in your own browser

You give it a goal — the field, the kind of firm, how many people. It works through that list one person at a time, in a Chrome window you can watch.

  1. 1

    Finds the right people

    Searches LinkedIn for professionals matching your goal and reads each profile to judge whether they are actually a fit.

  2. 2

    Verifies their email

    Looks up a real, verified work email. Anyone it cannot find a verified address for is skipped rather than guessed at.

  3. 3

    Writes and sends the intro

    Writes a short introduction grounded in your résumé and their background, then sends it from your own Gmail.

While it runs

You can see everything it is doing

It names the person it is working on right now and shows exactly how far through the list it is. Nothing happens off-screen.

Your accounts

It never sees your passwords

The agent asks for LinkedIn and Gmail, so it is worth being precise about what that actually means.

  • ✓ Passwords are never stored, or even seen. You log in yourself, once, in a real browser window. The app only keeps the resulting session — the same thing your browser keeps when you tick "remember me".
  • ✓ Everything stays on your Mac. Your résumé, your contacts and your sessions are stored locally. No account to create, nothing uploaded.
  • ✓ The browser is visible on purpose. You watch it search, read profiles and send — and you can stop it at any point.
  • ✓ Nobody is emailed twice. Everyone contacted is remembered across campaigns, so a new campaign will never write to the same person again.
Setup

About two minutes

  1. Download and install

    Open the .dmg and drag Recruiting Agent into Applications.

  2. Open it once

    If it opens normally, you are done — skip to the next step.

    If macOS says the app is damaged or can't be opened, that is expected on this beta: it is not yet notarized by Apple. Open Terminal and paste this once, then open the app again.
    xattr -cr "/Applications/Recruiting Agent.app"

  3. Install Google Chrome

    The agent drives its own separate Chrome window, kept apart from your everyday browsing. Get Chrome

  4. Set up your agent

    Add your name, school and résumé, describe who you want to reach, then connect LinkedIn and Gmail and press start.

Requirements

What you need

  • ✓ A Mac — Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 11 or later
  • ✓ Google Chrome installed
  • ✓ A LinkedIn account and a Gmail account you can log into
