How Orbit works

A networking pipeline, not another spreadsheet.

Give Orbit a goal and a little context. It handles the repetitive work one person at a time, leaving you with a visible trail of every decision and action.

  1. Set a precise target

    Choose the fields, firms, roles, location, and number of professionals you want to reach.

  2. Let Orbit research

    It evaluates real LinkedIn profiles and skips people who are not a meaningful fit.

  3. Send a human note

    Orbit verifies the email, writes from your background, and sends through your own Gmail.

Your accounts stay yours

Control is part of the product.

Orbit asks for LinkedIn and Gmail because that is where the work happens. It opens a real browser and lets you sign in yourself.

  • ✓
    Passwords are never stored or seen.

    You sign in directly with each service. Orbit only keeps the same local session your browser remembers.

  • ✓
    Your data stays on your computer.

    Your résumé, contacts, and account sessions are stored locally rather than uploaded.

  • ✓
    You can stop at any moment.

    The browser stays visible while Orbit searches, evaluates profiles, and sends.

From download to first campaign in minutes.

Orbit is available for macOS 11+ and Windows 10/11. Google Chrome, LinkedIn, and Gmail are required.

  1. Install Orbit macOS

    Open the .dmg and drag Orbit (Recruiting Agent) into Applications.

  2. Open it once

    If macOS says the beta is damaged or cannot be opened, run the command below in Terminal, then open it again.

    xattr -cr "/Applications/Recruiting Agent.app"

  1. Install Orbit Windows

    Run the .exe. If Windows protects your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway; this beta is not code-signed yet.

  1. Make sure Chrome is installed

    Orbit uses a separate Chrome window so its work stays apart from your everyday browsing. Get Chrome

  2. Create your Orbit profile

    Add your background and résumé, set a recruiting goal, connect LinkedIn and Gmail, then start your first campaign.
